Before you start

  • One trophy can be missed. Positive Mental Attitude may need Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 — do it the morning after Down to Earth. It is the only trophy any guide flags as possibly missable.
  • No mission replay, no New Game Plus. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.
  • Cheats switch trophies off. Keep cheats off for the whole run — they disable trophies and saving.
  • All 55 are single-player. There are no online trophies and no difficulty trophies. Around 200 hours to the platinum.
